Something I've always appreciated about you

You've never really changed. That's the part I appreciate most — not in a boring way, but in a way that feels rare. The trust, the honesty, the same nature and behaviour, whether it's been a good week or a bad one. People change with time, with mood, with convenience. You just didn't.

I don't think I've ever told you that this consistency is what made it easy to trust you in the first place. It's a quiet thing, easy to miss, but it's the foundation everything else was built on.

A memory I never told you was special

I don't even have one specific moment to point to — and that's kind of the point. It's every regular conversation. The random texts, the pointless calls, the "kya kar rahi ho" messages that turned into an hour of talking about nothing. Every single time, it made me feel special, and I never said it because it felt too small to mention.

Turns out the small things were never small.

Something about you that always makes me laugh

Your innocence. Genuinely. There's a certain baby-like cuteness in how you react to things — the way you get confused over the simplest stuff, or how seriously you take the smallest things. It's not something you're doing on purpose, which makes it funnier.

I could be having the worst day and one dumb reaction from you fixes my mood instantly.

A moment I'd happily relive

Honestly? The moments right after I've done something stupid — some "altu jalatu harkat" — and you forgive me anyway. Not because I enjoy messing up, but because that moment of being forgiven, that easy reset, is something I'd replay on loop if I could.

It says a lot about you that forgiving me is never a whole event. You just let it go, and we move on like nothing happened.

Something I've wanted to say

You are the best person I've ever met. I don't say it often because it feels like it should be obvious, but I don't want it to go unsaid — stay exactly the way you are. Don't let anything or anyone change that about you.

That's it. That's the whole message. Some things don't need three paragraphs to matter.

A random thought I've never told you

Random 2 a.m. thought: what if we'd never met? I try to imagine it sometimes and I genuinely can't picture finding another person like you — not a replacement, just someone who fits the same way you do. That thought alone makes me appreciate the version of life where we did meet.

Why this friendship matters to me

Because it's old. Because it's not "matlab ka rishta" — not based on convenience, not based on what either of us needs at a given moment. It's lasted through time without needing a reason to keep going. That's rare, and that's exactly why it matters.

Most things fade when they stop being useful. This one didn't, because it was never about being useful in the first place.
